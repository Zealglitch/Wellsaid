Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At WellSaid, we care deeply about user privacy, discretion, and interpersonal safety.

WellSaid Chrome Extension & Web App

The WellSaid Chrome extension operates locally in a secure sandbox in your browser:

  • No Permanent Storage of Messages: Your message drafts are processed locally or in memory solely to generate rewritten tone variations. We do not store, log, or harvest your personal communications.
  • Local Device Storage: The extension uses standard browser storage (chrome.storage.local) solely to preserve your active draft on your own device so you don't lose your text if you switch tabs. This data remains on your machine and is never transmitted to advertisers or third parties.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal communications or user data under any circumstances.

Payments

All transactions are handled securely by authorized payment partners. WellSaid never sees or stores full credit card numbers or financial account credentials.
