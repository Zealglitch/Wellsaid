Content Moderation & Safeguards Policy

Last updated: November 2026

WellSaid is designed exclusively to assist users with constructive, respectful, and clear interpersonal communication (such as polite declines, diplomatic feedback, professional boundary setting, and empathetic apologies). We enforce a strict zero-tolerance policy against prohibited, harmful, and abusive use.

1. Prohibited Content Categories

The following categories of content are strictly forbidden from being submitted, processed, or generated through WellSaid:

  • Harassment & Bullying: Derogatory remarks, cyberbullying, targeted emotional abuse, intimidation, stalking, or malicious defamation.
  • Hate Speech: Content that promotes violence, incites hatred, or attacks individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, age, veteran status, sexual orientation, gender identity, or other protected characteristics.
  • NSFW & Sexually Explicit Content: Sexually suggestive content, explicit pornography, non-consensual sexual content, solicitation, or any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Violence & Physical Harm: Direct or indirect threats of physical harm, incitement of self-harm, instructions for suicide, or weapons/explosives guidance.
  • Extortion, Fraud & Illegal Activity: Blackmail, fraudulent schemes, phishing templates, financial extortion, or facilitation of illegal activities.

2. Automated Detection & Prevention Safeguards

To detect, monitor, and restrict non-compliant content in real time, WellSaid implements a multi-tiered safety architecture:

  • Automated AI Safety Classifiers: All user inputs and rewritten outputs are evaluated against automated multi-modal safety filters powered by Google Gemini AI Safety Infrastructure. Inputs flagged for Hate Speech, Harassment, Dangerous Content, or Sexually Explicit themes are blocked prior to generation.
  • Pre-Execution Text Sanitization: Input messages are screened for malicious script injections, system prompt manipulation, and known threat patterns before reaching processing models.
  • Fail-Safe Generation Blocking: If an automated safety threshold is triggered at any point during generation, the request is immediately terminated and a generic error notice is displayed without outputting prohibited text.

3. Privacy & Data Minimization

WellSaid follows strict data protection and minimization principles. We do not permanently store or sell personal user message drafts. Original draft inputs are processed transiently in memory to generate phrasing suggestions and are not stored in any long-term persistent database.

4. Monitoring, Reporting & Enforcement

We actively monitor system telemetry to detect abuse anomalies and brute-force attempts. If any user or automated client attempts to circumvent safety filters or repeatedly submit prohibited content:

  • Their IP address and browser session will be temporarily or permanently blocked from the platform.
  • Any associated premium access or lifetime license will be revoked immediately without entitlement to a refund.
