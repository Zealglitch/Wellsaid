# WellSaid — Chrome & Edge Browser Extension

Say the hard thing, well — right inside your inbox, Gmail, Slack, and any web compose field.

## How to Install in 3 Steps:

1. Open Google Chrome, Brave, or Microsoft Edge.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions` in your address bar.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select this `extension` folder.

## How to Use:
1. Open Gmail, Slack, Outlook, LinkedIn, or any website with a text box.
2. Click into the message box or type a rough draft.
3. Click the floating `✍️` icon in the bottom-right of the box.
4. Choose the tone you want (*Warm & gentle*, *Direct & professional*, *Short & firm*).
5. Click **Replace in inbox** — your draft is instantly replaced with the polished version!
